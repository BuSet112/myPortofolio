About Me

I am a technology enthusiast with an academic background in Computer Systems and a strong interest in developing technology-based systems. I enjoy exploring the integration of hardware and software to create practical and innovative solutions.

My main areas of interest include Internet of Things (IoT), embedded systems, electronics, computer vision, and artificial intelligence. I have experience working with microcontrollers such as ESP32 and Arduino, sensors, Python programming, OpenCV, and YOLO-based object detection.

Through academic and personal projects, I have developed systems that combine computer vision and embedded technology, including real-time object detection using YOLO and IoT systems using ESP32 and sensors.

I am continuously improving my skills in programming, artificial intelligence, computer vision, and system development while building projects that can be applied to real-world problems.
